Log - a record of events that happen on any given system, like a computer recording that someone failed to log in at 2:00am.

SIEM - a tool used to collect logs from multiple different systems into to one place and then analyze them.

Alert - simply means that a suspicious pattern was flagged and someone should look into it.

False positive - an alert that turned out to be harmless, like an employee getting their password wrong multiple times.

Incident - is an alert that was properly escalated and confirmed to be a real security event, like an attacker gaining access to someone's account.
